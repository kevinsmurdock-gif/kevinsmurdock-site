Diversified Medical Healthcare

Diversified Medical Healthcare (DMH) is a Greenville-based holding entity. In its February 23, 2022 announcement, the company said it was founded in 2011 and that its companies were Premier Medical Laboratory Services, CPT Medical, Vessel Medical, and OnGen. The same release’s about section also named Dynasty Medical Laboratory Services and First Medical Laboratory Services among its laboratories. Corporate headquarters were listed at 6000 Pelham Road in Greenville.

That announcement described a $51 million investment and 185 new jobs in Greenville County, including a planned 136,000-square-foot facility at 315 Tanner Price Way, expected to be completed by 2025. It was issued on PRNewswire and published by the South Carolina governor’s office. In the same announcement, CEO Kevin Murdock said: “Our mission is to positively impact patient lives with solutions that drive the healthcare industry forward.” Gov. Henry McMaster and Commerce Secretary Harry M. Lightsey III issued statements congratulating the company on the expansion. The company also said that, over the prior two years, it had provided a testing capacity of over 100,000 tests per day with 98% of results delivered in under 24 hours, and that its companies served customers in all 50 U.S. states.
