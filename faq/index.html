Who is Kevin Murdock?

Kevin Murdock, formally Kevin S. Murdock, is a Greenville, South Carolina healthcare entrepreneur, a member of The Citadel Class of 1992, and the founder of Premier Medical Laboratory Services. The Citadel Today, citing WYFF News 4, reported in April 2020 that the laboratory was the first private commercial testing facility in South Carolina to receive FDA validation to test for COVID-19. What did Premier Medical Laboratory Services do during COVID-19?

In March 2020, WYFF News 4 and SCBiz reported that Premier Medical Laboratory Services in Greenville County was granted FDA validation to run COVID-19 tests, making it the first private commercial testing facility in South Carolina to do so. The Citadel Today later published the same account and noted that Kevin Murdock, the founder, is a member of The Citadel Class of 1992. On April 16, 2020, WIS-TV reported that the Greenville-based laboratory offered free COVID-19 tests to first responders in the state, including law enforcement, firefighters, EMTs, and paramedics, who believed they may have been exposed but did not otherwise qualify.

He is based in Greenville, South Carolina. The laboratory is in Greenville County. Diversified Medical Healthcare’s February 23, 2022 announcement listed its corporate headquarters at 6000 Pelham Road in Greenville.

What did Diversified Medical Healthcare announce in February 2022?

On February 23, 2022, Diversified Medical Healthcare announced a $51 million investment and 185 new jobs in Greenville County. The announcement said DMH was founded in 2011, identified Kevin Murdock as CEO, and named Premier Medical Laboratory Services, CPT Medical, Vessel Medical, and OnGen. The South Carolina governor’s office also published the announcement. The company said the expansion, including a planned 136,000-square-foot facility at 315 Tanner Price Way, was expected to be completed by 2025. See the companies page and press coverage.
