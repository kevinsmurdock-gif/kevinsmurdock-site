2022

South Carolina Governor’s Office

Diversified Medical Healthcare announced an expansion in Greenville County

The official announcement described a more than $51 million investment that would create 185 new jobs. It named Premier Medical Laboratory Services, CPT Medical, Vessel Medical, and OnGen, and quoted CEO Kevin Murdock.

February 23, 2022

The Post and Courier

Greenville medical firm adds 185 jobs, invests $51M in expansion

Greenville-based Diversified Medical Healthcare planned to expand into a new facility and launch another product line, an investment of $51 million that would create 185 new jobs. The article also states that Premier Medical Laboratory Services was the first lab in the state to be validated.

February 23, 2022

SCBiz

Diversified Medical Healthcare to invest $51 million, add 185 jobs

Greenville’s Diversified Medical Healthcare announced plans to invest $51 million in growth, an expansion that would mean 185 new professional jobs for the Upstate. The article quotes CEO Kevin Murdock and Gov. Henry McMaster.

February 23, 2022

2021

PRNewswire (company press release)

One Year Since FDA Validation of South Carolina’s First COVID-19 Testing Laboratory

In this company press release, Diversified Medical Healthcare marked one year since Premier Medical Laboratory Services was validated as an independent COVID-19 testing laboratory in South Carolina. The company reported more than one million COVID-19 tests processed, a laboratory staff of more than 360 employees, and that CPT Medical was capable of manufacturing 60,000 test kits per day.

March 23, 2021

PRNewswire (company press release)

Senator Tim Scott’s Senior Staff Visits SC’s First COVID-19 Testing Lab at One Year Mark

In this company press release, Diversified Medical Healthcare announced that Sen. Tim Scott’s senior staff visited the company’s Greenville headquarters, which houses Premier Medical Laboratory Services, CPT Medical, OnGen, and Vessel Medical. The release quotes Senator Scott calling the companies a valuable partner for South Carolina. It also states that the visit came just two weeks after SC State Representatives Bobby Cox and Bruce Bannister visited the facility.

April 8, 2021

SCBIO Foundation

Senator Tim Scott’s senior staff visited the Greenville laboratory

April 8, 2021

SCBiz

Sen. Tim Scott’s senior staff visited the Greenville laboratory

SCBiz reported that Sen. Tim Scott’s senior staff visited the 40,000-square-foot Greenville facility that houses Premier Medical Laboratory Services, CPT Medical, OnGen, and Vessel Medical. The article quoted Senator Scott, from a news release, saying the companies had been a valuable partner for South Carolina.

April 9, 2021

WYFF News 4

Greenville County lab among largest in country testing for COVID-19 variants

WYFF News 4 reported that Premier Medical Laboratory Services in Greenville County was among the largest laboratories in the country testing for COVID-19 variants. The station said the lab had added three genome sequencing machines to identify COVID-19 strains and was preparing to begin that testing.

April 21, 2021

Greenville Journal (sponsored/branded content)

Sen. Tim Scott’s staff visits Greenville medical lab

In this sponsored/branded content, published May 7, 2021, Diversified Medical Healthcare announced that Sen. Tim Scott’s staff visited the Greenville facility that houses Premier Medical Laboratory Services. The article says founder Kevin Murdock introduced them to the company’s research and development team.

May 7, 2021

The Post and Courier

Sen. Lindsey Graham toured Premier Medical Laboratories in Greenville

The Post and Courier reported that Sen. Lindsey Graham toured Premier Medical Laboratory Services in Greenville and held a press availability there. Kevin Murdock, CEO, appeared with him.

June 7, 2021

PRNewswire (company press release)

U.S. Senator Lindsey Graham Speaks on The Importance of Variant Tracking Amid Increasing Spread of the Delta Variant

In this company press release, Diversified Medical Healthcare said U.S. Sen. Lindsey Graham toured Premier Medical Laboratory Services in Greenville and discussed variant tracking. The release states that Senator Graham spoke alongside Kevin Murdock, CEO of Premier Medical Laboratory Services, and includes a photograph of Murdock showing Graham around the laboratory.

June 17, 2021

2020

SCBiz

COPING WITH COVID: CPT Medical

SCBiz reported that CPT Medical, in conjunction with Premier Medical Laboratory Services, expected to produce 54,000 COVID-19 test kits. The article quotes CPT Medical CEO and founder Connie Liesman on expanding domestic production.

March 23, 2020

SCBiz

Greenville lab validated for COVID-19 tests

Greenville-based Premier Medical Laboratory Services became the first commercial laboratory in the state validated to process COVID-19 testing, the company announced. The article quotes founder Kevin Murdock.

March 24, 2020

PRNewswire (company press release)

First Commercial Laboratory in South Carolina Validated for COVID-19 Testing

In this company press release, Premier Medical Laboratory Services, headquartered in Greenville, announced it was validated for COVID-19 testing. The release described a then-current testing capacity of 1,000 tests per day, with plans to reach 28,000 per week, and quoted founder Kevin Murdock.

March 24, 2020

WYFF News 4

Upstate commercial lab becomes first in SC to test for COVID-19

Premier Medical Laboratory Services in Greenville County was granted validation by the Food and Drug Administration to run tests for COVID-19, making it the first private commercial testing facility in South Carolina to do so. Founder Kevin Murdock said he planned to hire local people laid off from restaurants and businesses.

March 25, 2020

PRNewswire

The laboratory announced free COVID-19 tests for first responders and FDA EUA validation

April 3, 2020

WIS-TV

WIS-TV reported free COVID-19 testing for South Carolina first responders

A private lab based in Greenville offered free COVID-19 tests to first responders in the state, including law enforcement, firefighters, EMTs, and paramedics, who believed they may have been exposed but did not otherwise qualify. The report quotes Kevin Murdock, founder of Premier Medical Laboratory Services.

April 16, 2020

The Citadel Today

Citadel alum founded the first private commercial SC facility validated by the FDA to test for COVID-19

The Citadel Today notes that Kevin Murdock, the founder of Premier Medical Laboratory Services, is a member of The Citadel Class of 1992, and reports that the laboratory was granted FDA validation to run COVID-19 tests, making it the first private commercial testing facility in South Carolina to do so.

April 24, 2020

PRNewswire

Vessel Medical announced donations of N95 masks to South Carolina frontline workers

2020

SCBiz

Greenville companies partner to patch a hole in COVID-19 supply chain

SCBiz reported that Greenville companies CPT Medical and Hoowaki had begun working together on COVID-19 test kits. The article said CPT Medical had brought on 25 additional workers and aimed to produce about 200,000 test kits weekly once the operation was automated.

July 31, 2020
