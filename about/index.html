Biography

Healthcare Entrepreneur in Greenville, South Carolina

Kevin Murdock, formally Kevin S. Murdock, is a healthcare entrepreneur based in Greenville, South Carolina, and a member of The Citadel Class of 1992.

Kevin S. Murdock of Greenville, South Carolina, a member of The Citadel Class of 1992, is not the Miami-based professional of the same first and last name associated with Growth & Exit Partners, Stanford, or McKinsey.

He founded Premier Medical Laboratory Services in Greenville County. In April 2020, The Citadel Today, citing WYFF News 4, reported that the laboratory had been granted Food and Drug Administration validation to run tests for COVID-19, making it the first private commercial testing facility in South Carolina to do so.

In April 2020 the laboratory also announced free COVID-19 tests for first responders. WIS-TV reported that program, and a April 3, 2020 company announcement described the effort for EMTs, firefighters, and law enforcement.

Premier Medical Laboratory Services was one of the companies named in a February 23, 2022 announcement by Diversified Medical Healthcare, a holding entity the announcement said was founded in 2011. The other companies named in the body of that announcement were CPT Medical, Vessel Medical, and OnGen. Corporate headquarters were listed at 6000 Pelham Road in Greenville. The company announced a $51 million investment and 185 new jobs in Greenville County, including a planned 136,000-square-foot facility at 315 Tanner Price Way, expected to be completed by 2025. The South Carolina governor’s office published the announcement, and it was issued on PRNewswire. In that announcement, the company said that over the prior two years it had provided a testing capacity of over 100,000 tests per day, with 98% of results delivered in under 24 hours, and that its companies served customers in all 50 U.S. states.

Kevin continues to work in Greenville, South Carolina.

Laboratory Visits by Public Officials

Two visits to the Greenville laboratory are documented in independent or contemporaneous public accounts.

U.S. Sen. Lindsey Graham

On June 7, 2021, Sen. Lindsey Graham toured Premier Medical Laboratories in Greenville, as reported by The Post and Courier.

Senior staff of U.S. Sen. Tim Scott

On April 8, 2021, the SCBIO Foundation published an account of a visit to the Greenville headquarters by senior staff of Sen. Tim Scott. That account quoted Senator Scott as saying: “From leading research and innovation to generously donating PPE for our front line workers, Diversified Medical Healthcare has been a valuable partner for South Carolina. Their efforts highlight the life-saving impact of American innovation, and I am proud of the work they have done.” The same account described a workforce of more than 450 people at the time of the visit. That figure is from the 2021 visit write-up and is not presented here as a current headcount.
